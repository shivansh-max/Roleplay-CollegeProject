01 / OVERVIEW

The Opportunity That Wasn't What It Seemed.

A reputed company arrives on campus with what appears to be an excellent career opportunity. The package sounds attractive, the job role looks promising, and students see it as one of the biggest opportunities of their college life.

After multiple rounds of interviews, several students are selected. Excitement spreads across the campus, and some students begin making important career decisions based on what they believe the offer represents.

However, when the official offer letters finally arrive, important details begin to raise questions. The compensation structure, job conditions, and expectations are more complicated than the students originally understood.

What follows is not simply an argument between students and a company. It becomes a realistic professional conflict involving communication, responsibility, transparency, career decisions, and the consequences of assumptions.

SCENE 01

The Big Opportunity

🎭 SETTING: College placement presentation.
⏸️ LIGHTS ON. Narrator stands to one side.

NARRATOR: Every year, thousands of students wait for one particular moment.

NARRATOR: The moment when a company visits their campus.

NARRATOR: For some, it is simply another opportunity. For others, it feels like the beginning of their career.

NARRATOR: Today, a reputed company has arrived at the college for campus recruitment.

⏸️ Pause for 2 seconds.

PLACEMENT OFFICER: Good morning, everyone. Today, we are pleased to welcome the recruitment team conducting today's campus placement drive.

PLACEMENT OFFICER: I request everyone to listen carefully, participate professionally, and use this opportunity to understand the recruitment process.

PLACEMENT OFFICER: Now, I would like to invite the company representatives to begin the presentation.

🚶 HR Representative steps forward.

HR REPRESENTATIVE: Good morning, everyone. It's great to see so many enthusiastic students here today.

HR REPRESENTATIVE: We are looking for talented graduates who are ready to learn, adapt and grow in a professional environment.

HR REPRESENTATIVE: The selected candidates will get opportunities to work on real projects and build their careers with our organisation.

RECRUITMENT MANAGER: Starting your professional career is a major step. We are looking for candidates who are technically capable, adaptable and responsible.

👀 Selected students react quietly.

SELECTED STUDENT 1: This actually sounds like a really good opportunity.

SELECTED STUDENT 2: If everything goes well, this could seriously change our plans for the future.

SELECTED STUDENT 3: Let's first get selected.

⏸️ Short natural laugh.

NARRATOR: The recruitment process begins. Aptitude tests. Technical interviews. HR rounds.

NARRATOR: And finally... the results are ready.

🎬 LIGHTS DIM • ACTORS CHANGE POSITION • 3 SECOND PAUSE
SCENE 02

The Celebration

🎭 SETTING: College corridor after results.

SELECTED STUDENT 1: I still can't believe it. We're actually selected!

SELECTED STUDENT 2: After all those rounds, I honestly thought I wouldn't make it.

SELECTED STUDENT 3: But we did. This calls for a celebration!

STUDENT REPRESENTATIVE: Congratulations! The entire batch is talking about the results.

SELECTED STUDENT 1: Honestly, this feels like a huge relief.

SELECTED STUDENT 2: My family was waiting for the result too. I already told them.

⏸️ Pause.

SELECTED STUDENT 1: I was preparing for other opportunities as well, but now I think I'll focus on this.

UNSELECTED STUDENT: Well, congratulations to all of you. Some of us weren't lucky enough to get selected.

SELECTED STUDENT 3: Hey, don't say that. There will be other opportunities.

UNSELECTED STUDENT: Maybe. But right now, you people should enjoy your success.

STUDENT REPRESENTATIVE: The offer letters should arrive soon. Then everything becomes official.

SELECTED STUDENT 2: Once the offer letter comes, I can finally plan properly.

NARRATOR: The students celebrate. Families are informed. Future plans begin to change.

NARRATOR: But sometimes, the most important information is found later... in the details.

🎬 LIGHTS DIM • 3 SECOND PAUSE
SCENE 03

The Offer Letter

🎭 SETTING: Students reading their official offer letters.

SELECTED STUDENT 1: Guys... did you all get the offer letter?

SELECTED STUDENT 2: Yeah. I just received mine.

SELECTED STUDENT 3: Same.

📄 All pretend to read.

⏸️ Short silence.

SELECTED STUDENT 1: Wait. Can someone explain this part?

SELECTED STUDENT 3: Which part?

SELECTED STUDENT 1: The compensation structure. This isn't exactly what I understood during the presentation.

SELECTED STUDENT 2: Let me see... Wait. You're right.

SELECTED STUDENT 1: During the presentation, the overall package was highlighted. But these details weren't explained clearly.

SELECTED STUDENT 3: Maybe we should read everything carefully before assuming anything.

SELECTED STUDENT 2: Terms and conditions are normal. But important conditions should be communicated clearly.

STUDENT REPRESENTATIVE: What's going on? I've received messages from several selected students. They all seem confused.

UNSELECTED STUDENT: So now that you've read the actual offer letter, you're complaining?

SELECTED STUDENT 1: We're not complaining because we have to work. We're questioning whether important details were communicated properly.

SELECTED STUDENT 2: Imagine making an important decision because you understood something in a particular way... and later discovering the complete situation is different.

STUDENT REPRESENTATIVE: Enough. Let's not argue among ourselves. We should speak to the Placement Officer professionally.

🎬 ACTORS MOVE TO PLACEMENT CELL • 3 SECOND PAUSE
SCENE 04

The Placement Cell

🎭 SETTING: Placement Officer and Faculty Coordinator meeting students.

PLACEMENT OFFICER: I've received multiple messages regarding the offer letters. Before we contact the company, I want to understand the issue clearly. One person at a time.

STUDENT REPRESENTATIVE: The students are concerned that important aspects of the offer were not clearly understood during the presentation.

PLACEMENT OFFICER: Which specific information do you believe was unclear?

SELECTED STUDENT 1: The way the package was presented created a particular understanding. But after reading the offer letter, the structure and conditions are much more complicated.

SELECTED STUDENT 2: Some of us made important decisions based on this opportunity.

FACULTY COORDINATOR: Let's be careful with the language we use. There is a difference between saying information was deliberately hidden and saying communication was unclear.

SELECTED STUDENT 1: You're right. We're not accusing anyone of intentionally deceiving us. We're saying important information should have been clearer.

SELECTED STUDENT 3: I still think the opportunity has value. The conditions may not be ideal, but that doesn't necessarily mean the company did something wrong.

SELECTED STUDENT 2: That's your decision. But shouldn't every student be able to make that decision with complete information?

⏸️ Brief pause.

FACULTY COORDINATOR: That's a fair question.

PLACEMENT OFFICER: I will arrange a meeting with the company representatives. But remember, this meeting must remain professional. No shouting. No personal attacks. We focus on facts.

🎬 LIGHTS DIM • MEETING TABLE SETUP • 5 SECOND PAUSE
SCENE 05

The Confrontation

🎭 SETTING: Formal meeting room.
👥 Students on one side • Company representatives on the other • College officials in the centre.

PLACEMENT OFFICER: Thank you, everyone, for attending. The purpose of this meeting is to discuss concerns regarding the communication surrounding the recruitment offer.

PLACEMENT OFFICER: I request everyone to speak respectfully and allow others to finish.

STUDENT REPRESENTATIVE: The students' primary concern is not that the company has employment conditions. Their concern is that important details became clear only after the official offer letters were received.

HR REPRESENTATIVE: We understand the concern. However, the official offer letter contains the complete terms of employment. Candidates are expected to read the document carefully before accepting.

SELECTED STUDENT 1: We understand that. And we did read the document carefully. That's exactly why we're asking these questions.

SELECTED STUDENT 1: If a major part of the decision depends on conditions written later in the offer letter, shouldn't those conditions also be clearly discussed earlier?

RECRUITMENT MANAGER: The pre-placement presentation is intended to provide an overview. The official offer letter provides the complete details. This is a standard professional process.

SELECTED STUDENT 2: Standard doesn't always mean clear.

⏸️ Important pause.

SELECTED STUDENT 2: I informed my family. I changed my plans. I even considered stopping preparation for other opportunities.

SELECTED STUDENT 2: If I had known every important condition earlier, I might still have accepted the offer... or I might have made a different decision. But at least it would have been an informed decision.

FACULTY COORDINATOR: That is an important distinction. The issue is not whether the company is allowed to have conditions. The issue is whether candidates had enough clarity before making decisions.

UNSELECTED STUDENT: I wasn't selected. And honestly, some students might still be willing to accept this offer even after knowing all the conditions.

SELECTED STUDENT 3: I agree. The communication could have been clearer. But I don't think the solution is necessarily to reject the opportunity.

SELECTED STUDENT 1: Nobody is forcing anyone to reject it. We're asking for transparency. Those are two different things.

⏸️ 2 second pause.

RECRUITMENT MANAGER: Fair enough. Perhaps we should acknowledge something from our side.

RECRUITMENT MANAGER: A presentation can sometimes focus heavily on opportunities and benefits. Important details may receive less attention than they deserve.

HR REPRESENTATIVE: If students genuinely felt that certain aspects were unclear, we can take that feedback seriously.

SELECTED STUDENT 2: That's all we wanted. Not an argument. Just acknowledgment that communication affects people's decisions.

FACULTY COORDINATOR: Students must ask questions. Companies must communicate clearly. And colleges must ensure that important information is properly understood.

🎬 MOOD BECOMES CALMER • SHORT PAUSE
SCENE 06

The Resolution

🎭 SETTING: Same meeting room. The atmosphere is calmer.

PLACEMENT OFFICER: So let's focus on practical solutions. What can be done now?

RECRUITMENT MANAGER: We can arrange a clarification session for all selected candidates. The company can explain the complete compensation structure, job expectations and important conditions.

STUDENT REPRESENTATIVE: That would definitely help. And for future placement drives, important terms should be clearly mentioned during the presentation itself.

HR REPRESENTATIVE: Agreed. We can also provide a written summary of key terms before candidates make their final decisions.

SELECTED STUDENT 3: That sounds fair. People can then decide for themselves.

SELECTED STUDENT 2: Some may accept. Some may not. But at least everyone will know what they're choosing.

UNSELECTED STUDENT: And maybe students should learn something too. Don't make a major career decision based only on an exciting presentation. Ask questions. Read everything.

SELECTED STUDENT 1: Because in the professional world, communication is not just about speaking. It's about making sure the other person truly understands.

FACULTY COORDINATOR: Clear communication prevents confusion. Responsible communication builds trust. And informed communication helps people make better decisions.

PLACEMENT OFFICER: Then I believe we have a constructive outcome.

PLACEMENT OFFICER: The company will provide clarification. Students will have the opportunity to make informed decisions. And in future placement drives, we will work toward greater transparency.

🎭 Everyone nods. Narrator slowly walks to the front.

NARRATOR: This was never simply a story about a bad placement.

NARRATOR: It was a story about communication. About expectations. About assumptions. And about the consequences of information that is not completely understood.

NARRATOR: In the professional world, words can influence decisions. A presentation can create expectations. An unclear statement can create confusion.

⏸️ Pause.

NARRATOR: And sometimes, the difference between a successful opportunity and a serious conflict...

⏸️ Pause for 2 seconds.

NARRATOR: ...is simply clear communication.

👥 All actors look toward the audience.

ALL: Thank you.

🎬 LIGHTS OFF • END OF ROLEPLAY
